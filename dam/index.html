A toy, not a dam safety assessment. Real: P = ρgQH, the forces on a gravity dam (water pressure, uplift, weight) and the sliding check, foundation drains to cut uplift, weir and gate flow, overtopping scouring the toe, the dam-break outflow, shallow-water flood waves, water hammer (Joukowsky and Michaud) against overspeed after a load rejection. Real failures: St Francis (1928, a gravity dam that failed at its foundation) and Teton (1976). Made up: the dam, the site, the tiny reservoir and the sped-up time, every coefficient, the town, and failure picked to happen on cue.
