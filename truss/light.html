A toy, not a bridge design. Real: the stiffness method for a pin-jointed truss, tension and compression, Euler buckling for compression members, Pratt against Howe, and a determinate truss becoming a mechanism. Typical: B-double 62.5 t, triple road train about 120 t, a big loaded mining haul truck about 620 t. Made up: the member sizes, the dead load, the speeds, no impact factor, and the sag drawn 80 times bigger (dead-load sag left out, as if the bridge were cambered).
