A toy, not a battery safety study. Roughly real, and it varies a lot from cell to cell: SEI breakdown from about 70 to 90 °C, PE separators melting around 130 to 140 °C, runaway around 150 to 200 °C for nickel and cobalt cathodes and about 250 °C for LFP, hot spots near 800 °C, 4.2 V max for NMC and about 3.6 V for LFP, and less charge meaning less energy. Made up: every rate, the heat model, how it spreads, the overcharge curve and the timings, which are squashed so you don't wait an hour.
