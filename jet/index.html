A toy, not an engine model. Real (GE9X, from GE Aerospace and Key Aero): 3.4 m fan, 16 carbon-fibre composite blades, a 3-stage booster, an 11-stage high-pressure compressor at 27:1, CMC combustor liners, a 2-stage HP turbine, a 6-stage low-pressure turbine, bypass about 10:1, overall pressure ratio 60:1, about 100,000 lbf (445 kN) take-off, and the 134,300 lbf (597 kN) record. Also real: certification needs a fan blade released at full power to stay inside the case, and the engine has to take bird strikes. Made up: the spool times, EGT numbers and limit, the thrust curve, how the failures play out, the stand, and the proportions inside are simplified.
