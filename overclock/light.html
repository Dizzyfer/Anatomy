A toy, not a tuning guide. Real: power going with voltage squared times clock, leakage rising with temperature, the voltage wall, cold pushing it out, liquid nitrogen at -196 °C, the silicon lottery, the cold bug, throttling and a thermal trip at the top end, over-current protection on the VRM, voltage droop under load, CLOCK_WATCHDOG_TIMEOUT and WHEA blue screens, and high voltage plus heat wearing a chip out. The real frequency record is about 9.2 GHz, set in 2026 on liquid helium. Made up: the chip, every number in the model, the score, and the time scale (temperatures settle in seconds, a render takes eight). Don't put 1.65 V through your own CPU.
