A toy, not a motor study. Anchored on a WEG W22 IE3 7.5 kW 4-pole (132M): 14.2 A, 1460 rpm, locked rotor current 8.3 × In, locked rotor torque 2.4 × Tn, breakdown 3.5 × Tn, class F, locked rotor time 12 s cold. Class 10 overload: trips at 7.2 × setting in 4 to 10 s. Made up: the circuit fitted to those ratios, the loads and their inertia, the timers, the drive, the supply dip, the thermal model and when it burns.
