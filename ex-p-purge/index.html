Ex p, purge and pressure

Tap: open the door

Ex p lets you put ordinary sparking kit in Zone 1 by keeping the gas out with clean air. First the cabinet is purged: at least five of its own volumes of air, because it never mixes perfectly. Then it's held above the zone at 50 Pa or more, so anything that leaks, leaks out. For px, losing the pressure cuts the power, and it stays off until it's purged again. It fails when someone powers it before the purge, bypasses the interlock and opens the door live, or takes the purge air from inside the zone. That last one fools the interlock completely: it can count volumes and read pressure, but it can't smell the air.

A toy, not a simulator. Real: IEC 60079-2, px: at least 50 Pa overpressure, a pre-purge of at least five enclosure volumes, power off on loss of pressure until a fresh purge, purge air from the safe area. Made up: the gas levels, how well it mixes, flows and timings.
