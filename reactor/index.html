A toy, not a reactor simulator. Real: point kinetics with delayed neutrons and the one-dollar prompt-critical line, Doppler feedback, the RBMK's positive void coefficient at low power and low margin against a PWR's negative one, xenon-135 piling up after a power cut, the RBMK's graphite displacers adding reactivity at the bottom of the core when AZ-5 drives in rods that were fully out (the positive scram), decay heat, and containment. The 1986 sequence follows World Nuclear Association's timeline (30 MWt, back to 200 MWt, margin at 8 rods against a minimum of 15, emergency cooling isolated, test at 01:23:04, AZ-5 at 01:23:40). Three Mile Island (1979) was a meltdown that the containment held. Made up: one delayed group and every coefficient, the sped-up xenon, decay heat and melting, the geometry and the explosion.
