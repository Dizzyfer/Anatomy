A toy, not a fault study. Real: 400/230 V three-phase four-wire, the AS 60038 range of 230 V +10 %/−6 % (216 to 253 V), the floating star point (Millman's theorem), and typical driver input ranges of 198 to 264 V (narrow) and 90 to 305 V (wide range, Mean Well HLG style). Made up: 150 W fittings treated as fixed loads while running (real constant-power drivers can make it worse), where drivers drop out, how fast they die above their rating, the MOV failing short and tripping the row, and the loose joint's behaviour.
