A toy, not gas safety advice. Real: methane 5 to 15 %, propane about 2.1 to 9.5 %, natural gas lighter than air and LPG heavier, odourised to be smelt at about a fifth of the LEL, natural gas detectors high and LPG ones low. Made up: the room, the leak rates, how it layers and mixes, the ventilation, the alarm level, the time-lapse (two and a half minutes a second) and the blast. If you smell gas: don't touch a switch, get out, call it in from outside.
